# Changelog

## 0.1.2

- The charter picker groups opening benefits and later costs on review. The Back prompt uses one native glyph or its text fallback.
- The debt badge shows the kingdom's owed material icon alongside the remaining balance.

## 0.1.1 (Local Test Build)

- The material debt badge clears the visible unit cards at the bottom of the HUD.

## 0.1.0

- Added the run-start picker, review screen, controller navigation and current-binding prompts.
- Added the Merchant, Forester and Mason charters, Standard Run, run-save persistence and a remaining-debt indicator.
- Preserved charter selection through day-one restarts and tied tavern restoration to the selected checkpoint.
- Added native confirmation of kingdom-specific supply terms and disclosed other mods’ caravan scheduling in the picker.
- Added challenge bypass and Daily Caravan compatibility.
- Preserved other starting grants, native material mappings and shop prices. Prepaid commission deliveries are free, including after deferral.
